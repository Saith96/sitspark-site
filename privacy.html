Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

Pawsitive does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your dog’s name, age group, goal, breed (if you enter it), lesson progress and session notes are stored only on your device. We cannot see them. If you delete the app or use Settings → Erase all my data, they are removed.

AI features

The AI trainer, behaviour plans and session tips use Apple’s on-device language model through Apple Intelligence. Your questions and answers are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us or to any server run by us.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Pawsitive schedules local notifications on your device. They are not sent from a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

Pawsitive is for dog owners and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: saithdevelopments@gmail.com.
