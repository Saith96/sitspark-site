Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026.

In short: Sitspark has no accounts, no servers and no ads, and no analytics service. We do not collect your data, and your dog’s information stays on your device. On Android, two Google libraries inside the app send Google limited technical data, never your dog’s information or any sound (see AI features and Microphone).

What stays on your device

Your dog’s name, age group, birth date (if you enter it), goal, breed (if you enter it), lesson progress and difficulty levels, session notes, potty log, care calendar (vet visits, vaccinations, medication, grooming), socialisation log, alone-time sessions and the dates your dog finished each class are stored only on your iPhone or Android phone. We cannot see them. If you delete the app or use Settings → Erase all my data, they are removed. On Android they are left out of Google cloud backup; a direct transfer to your own new phone can include them.

On iPhone, Sitspark also keeps a small count of the free AI questions you have used in your device’s Keychain, so that erasing your data does not hand out more free questions. It contains no personal information and stays on your device.

AI features

The AI trainer, behaviour plans, session tips and the weekly coach’s note run on your device: Apple’s on-device language model through Apple Intelligence on iPhone, and Gemini Nano on supported Android phones. Your questions and answers are processed on your phone and are not sent to us or to any server run by us. If you choose to report AI text, your e-mail app opens with that text filled in, and we receive it only if you send the e-mail. The first time you use the AI on Android, your phone may download the model from Google.

Android only: Gemini Nano is reached through Google’s ML Kit. According to Google, ML Kit sends Google device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics and error codes, for diagnostics and usage analytics. We do not receive this data. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

Usage counts (Android)

To see which parts of the app help, Sitspark counts a few things on your phone, such as how many sessions you saved, how often the Plus page was opened and on which day each first happened. It never counts what you type, your dog’s details or your notes. The counts stay on your phone. In Settings → Help improve Sitspark you can read all of them, and if you choose to send them, your e-mail app opens with them filled in; we receive them only if you send the e-mail. Erase all my data removes them.

Microphone (bark monitor)

The bark monitor is used while you practise leaving your dog alone. Only while its screen is open, it listens through the microphone and sound recognition on your phone tells barking, whining and howling apart, about once a second: Apple’s built-in sound recognition on iPhone, and a sound-recognition model inside the app on Android. The sound is never recorded, kept or sent anywhere: only the times when your dog made a sound are saved on your phone, so you can see how long they stayed calm. Your phone asks for microphone permission the first time you start it, and you can turn it off in your phone’s settings at any time.

Android only: the model runs through Google’s MediaPipe library. According to Google, MediaPipe sends Google metrics about how the library performs and is used in the app, to measure, maintain and improve it. The sound itself is processed on your phone and is not sent. We do not receive this data.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Sitspark schedules local notifications on your device. If you start a free trial, it also schedules one reminder the day before the trial ends. They are not sent from a server.

Sharing, backups and summaries

The progress card and the certificate for a finished class are created on your device and go only where you choose to share them.

In Settings you can create a backup file of your data, or a PDF summary of your dog’s training notes and logs for a vet or trainer. Both are created on your device and go only where you choose in the share sheet, for example Files, iCloud Drive or Mail. We never receive them. A backup you restore replaces the data on that phone.

Siri and shortcuts

On iPhone, Sitspark offers shortcuts such as starting the clicker or logging a potty event. They run in the app on your device. What you say to Siri is handled by Apple under Apple’s own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple on iPhone and by Google Play on Android. We do not receive your payment details. Their own privacy policies apply to purchases.

Children

Sitspark is for dog owners and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
