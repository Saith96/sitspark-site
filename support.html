Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription? On iPhone: open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Sitspark. On Android: open the Google Play app → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Sitspark. You can also use Settings inside Sitspark → Manage subscription.

I bought Plus but it is locked. Open Sitspark → Settings → Restore purchases.

The AI trainer says it needs Apple Intelligence. It runs on your device. On iPhone, turn on Apple Intelligence in Settings on a supported model. On Android, it needs a phone that supports Gemini Nano, and other phones do not show it. Everything else works without it.

Is the training advice from a vet? No. It is general guidance. For aggression, pain or sudden changes in behaviour, see a vet or a certified trainer.
