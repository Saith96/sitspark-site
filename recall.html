Teach your dog to come when called

A recall is a habit of turning towards you because it has always paid off. Build it where it is easy, and protect the word from ever meaning 'the fun is over'.

1. Choose a word only for recall

Such as 'Here!'. If 'Come' already means 'maybe later' to your dog, start fresh with a new word.

2. Use extra tasty treats

Small pieces of chicken or cheese, kept for recall alone.

3. Say it in a happy voice, then step backward

Moving away draws your dog in. Reward the moment they reach you.

4. Let your dog go back to play

Recall must not always end the fun. Call, reward, release, many times on every walk.

5. Use a long line outdoors

Don't let your dog off the leash in open areas before the recall is reliable.

By breed group. Sighthounds can sprint after anything that moves, and scent hounds may not hear you over a smell: use a long line or a fenced field and treat off-leash as a risk, not a goal.

Never call your dog to you for something they dislike, such as a bath or a telling-off. Go and fetch them instead.
