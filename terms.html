What the app is

Sitspark gives general dog-training guidance, including tips and plans from on-device AI. It is not a substitute for a vet or a qualified dog trainer or behaviourist, and AI answers can be wrong. Keep your dog safe while training: use a lead near traffic, never force a dog into a situation it is afraid of, and stop if your dog shows pain or stress. For aggression, biting, sudden behaviour changes or any health worry, see a vet or a qualified professional.
