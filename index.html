Sitspark

Calm, reward-based dog training in short daily sessions. Your first class and the first lesson of every class are free. No account needed.

  • 8 classes and 36 step-by-step lessons for puppies and adult dogs, from first commands to tricks and everyday life
  • An animated Shiba Inu shows every lesson, and a hands-free step player reads the steps aloud
  • Difficulty levels for distance, duration and distraction, so each skill holds up anywhere
  • A puppy socialisation tracker and a bark monitor for practising time alone
  • Tips for nine breed groups, from herding dogs to flat-faced dogs
  • A fast clicker, a session timer and a session log
  • Skill progress, a daily streak with rest days, a weekly coach, certificates to share and gentle reminders
  • Plus: a private AI trainer and 7-day behaviour plans that run on your device, with Apple Intelligence on iPhone and Gemini Nano on supported Android phones

All methods are force-free. Sitspark gives general training guidance and is not veterinary advice.
